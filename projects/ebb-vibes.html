    Scenography.

    Slow z-axis descent. FOV shifts 52mm → 85mm. Minimal PC shake to keep distance.

    Material
    MeshStandardMaterial with warm emissive + roughness 0.6. No reflections — diffuse glow.

    Exponential fog (#cc8600 base). Peak opacity at 40% scroll.
